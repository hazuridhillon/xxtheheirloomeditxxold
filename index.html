An heirloom is meant to be worn.

The Heirloom Edit is a jewelry house for South Asian families abroad, devoted to one thing: turning the gold you inherited into pieces you'll live in.

Every family has a drawer like this.

Bangles too heavy for an ordinary day. A box that only opens for weddings. A slip of paper with a weight written on it in someone's hand.

I don't want it kept in a locker. I want to wear it on an ordinary Tuesday.

The sentence behind nearly every inquiry.

What the move left behind

In India, Pakistan and Bangladesh, remaking family jewelry is part of ordinary life. A grandmother's heavy set becomes a daughter's wedding necklace; a worn bangle becomes a pair of earrings. You go to a jeweler who knows your family, the gold is weighed in front of you, and the work begins.

When families moved abroad, the jewelry came with them. The jeweler didn't. The pieces went into lockers and velvet boxes, too formal for daily life and too precious to hand to a stranger.

A name worth trusting

Skilled goldsmiths work in every major city. What's been missing is a house that families recognize and trust with something irreplaceable: one that understands kundan and polki, respects what a piece means, and designs for how you actually live now.

That is what we are building. We guide the whole process, from the first photograph to the finished piece, and we work with master goldsmiths who have spent their lives on this craft.

Questions, answered

What families most often ask before trusting us with a piece.

Is my gold safe while it's with you?

Yes. From the moment a piece is in our care, it is insured, including while it travels. Every handover is documented with photographs and a recorded weight, so you always know exactly where your piece is and what condition it's in.

Will I get the same gold back?

Yes. Your piece is weighed and photographed before any work begins, and you receive a written record of its weight before and after. If gold is added, or if any is removed in the redesign, it is written down and discussed with you in advance.

How long does it take, and what does it cost?

Every piece is different, so we quote after the consultation, once we've seen the piece and agreed on a direction. Restorations are often quicker; redesigns typically take several weeks from approved design to finished piece. Your quote will include a timeline.

Can you keep the original design?

Absolutely. Restoration keeps a piece as it was: resetting loose stones, rebuilding clasps, cleaning and refinishing. In a redesign, we can also carry forward a signature element, such as a motif, a stone or a clasp, while changing the rest.

Do I need to live near you?

No. We work with families across the country by appointment. Consultations can happen virtually, and pieces travel by fully insured shipping.
